## 1. Core Principles & Non-Negotiable Rules

1. **Empirical Verifiability:** Every reported metric, parameter count, and evaluation result must be produced by an executed script with saved stdout logs, seeds, and configs in `results/logs/`. Zero numbers may be invented, estimated, hardcoded, or transcribed from previous work.
2. **Pre-Registration Commitment:** This protocol (`results/PROTOCOL.md`) defines the model ladder, spatial folds, tuning grids, feature definitions, and decision rules. It is committed before training models. Any unavoidable divergence must be logged with technical rationale in `results/DEVIATIONS.md`.
3. **Absence of Synthetic or Formula-Based Features:** All features must originate from verified Earth Observation rasters, topographic models, or mapped geospatial layers. Previous analytical formulas (such as `sar_vv_vh_ratio = -14.0 + 0.15*slope + noise`, `pre_event_ndvi = 0.75 - 0.008*slope + ...`, and formulaic rainfall/fault distance) are strictly banned.
4. **Prevention of Label & Temporal Leakage:**
   - No features may be derived from event labels, post-event imagery, or distance to known landslide scars.
   - Pre-event imagery only is permitted for EO predictors (NDVI and SAR backscatter).
   - Circular "weak positives" (generated by filtering coordinates based on model input features like slope and lineament distance) are strictly excluded from headline training and evaluation datasets.
5. **Out-of-Fold Spatial Block Cross-Validation:**
   - All headline metrics are out-of-fold (OOF) predictions from spatial block CV using 10 km $\times$ 10 km grid blocks.
   - Each landslide event cluster is constrained entirely within a single block.
   - Probability calibrators (Platt scaling, Isotonic regression) and feature scalers must be fitted strictly on inner training blocks—never on held-out test blocks.
6. **Reporting Bias Control:**
   - Distance to roads and railways is prohibited from headline susceptibility models. It is evaluated exclusively in a designated "reporting-bias control" model.
   - Headline models are evaluated against both unbiased random background negatives and a bias-matched background negative variant matching the infrastructure distance distribution of positives.
7. **Accurate Terminology:**
   - A tabular Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) adapted with LoRA (~4,500 parameters) is strictly designated as `SimpleTabularLoRAModel`.
   - The term **"Geospatial Foundation Model"** is reserved exclusively for pretrained vision-transformer models (e.g., `ibm-nasa-geospatial/Prithvi-EO-1.0-100M`) whose checkpoint weights and SHA-256 hashes are verifiably loaded from a public repository.
8. **Pre-Registered Decision Rule:**
   - A ladder rung $L_k$ is declared an **"Improvement"** over the preceding best rung $L_{best}$ if and only if the 95% block-bootstrap confidence interval of their paired difference ($\Delta \text{PR-AUC} = \text{PR-AUC}_{L_k} - \text{PR-AUC}_{L_{best}}$) strictly excludes zero ($\text{CI}_{lower} > 0$).
   - Otherwise, the rung is classified as **"Comparable"** (if zero is included) or **"Worse"** (if $\text{CI}_{upper} < 0$).
   - If an environmental or computational requirement (e.g., GPU or network) is missing, that rung is marked **`NOT RUN`** with explicit rationale.

## 2. Dataset & Inventory Specification (Step 0)

### 2.1 Geographic Domain & Topographic Model
- **Study Area Extent:** Araku Valley, Eastern Ghats highlands, Alluri Sitharama Raju (ASR) district, Andhra Pradesh, India.
- **Elevation Layer:** Real Copernicus GLO-30 Digital Elevation Model (`araku_dem_30m_real.tif`), 1 arcsecond (~30 m native spatial resolution, $3,600 \times 7,200$ cells, 25.92 million pixels).
- **Coordinate Reference System:** EPSG:4326 (WGS84).

### 2.2 Landslide Positives Inventory
- **Target Inventory:** Minimum 30 verified historical landslide events mined from multilingual news archives (Eenadu Telugu, The Hindu, Deccan Chronicle, Times of India), NDMA/GSI disaster damage reports, NASA Global Landslide Catalog (GLC/COOLR), and the Unified Global Landslide Catalogue (UGLC).
- **Verification Evidence:**
  - Date and localized toponym coordinates.
  - Multi-temporal satellite change verification using pre- and post-event imagery:
    - Sentinel-2 optical $\Delta\text{NDVI} \ge 0.15$
    - Sentinel-1 SAR backscatter decrease $\Delta\sigma^0 \le -1.5\,\text{dB}$
  - Documented in `results/events.csv` with STAC scene IDs, source URLs, triggers, dates, and coordinates.
- **Footprint Representation:** Multi-pixel footprint polygons (scars mapped on steep terrain $\ge 15^\circ$ directly corresponding to the failure zone; recorded as contiguous pixels per event).

### 2.3 Negatives (Pseudo-Absences)
- **Primary Set (Random Background):** Unbiased random background sampling drawn uniformly across the study area ($N_{neg} \ge 20 \times N_{pos}$), enforcing a strict exclusion buffer ($\ge 1,000\,\text{m}$) around every verified failure.
- **Bias-Matched Variant:** Background negatives sampled to match the empirical road/rail distance distribution of verified landslide events (to control for infrastructure reporting bias).
- **Hard Negatives Variant:** Steep stable terrain ($\text{slope} \ge 15^\circ$) located $> 1,000\,\text{m}$ from known failures.

### 2.4 Large Public Landslide Pretraining Dataset
- **Pretraining Source:** `ibm-nasa-geospatial/Landslide4sense` (or UGLC global inventory).
- Recorded with official license, sample size, and input channel definitions.

## 4. The Model Ladder (L0 to L5b)

Each rung introduces exactly ONE hypothesis or architectural idea. All rungs are evaluated across the identical spatial folds and evaluation protocol.

### Rung L0: Baseline Prior Only (National Atlas / Reference)
- **Concept:** Uncalibrated prior susceptibility sampled directly from the Geological Survey of India (GSI) National Landslide Susceptibility Mapping (NLSM/ILSM) atlas or regional macro-zonation.
- **Training:** Zero parameters trained. Evaluated directly as a reference benchmark.
- **Status:** If official GSI NLSM raster is unavailable for sampling, marked **`NOT RUN (Data Unavailable)`**.

### Rung L1: Pure Topographic Baseline (Linear & Additive)
- **Concept:** Can simple topography alone predict landslide locations under spatial cross-validation?
- **Features:** 16 clean Copernicus GLO-30 DEM derivatives (`elevation`, `slope`, `aspect_sin`, `aspect_cos`, curvatures, `twi`, relief, and $3\times3$ local patch statistics).
- **Models:**
  1. Regularized Logistic Regression ($L_2$ penalty, $C \in [10^{-3}, 10^2]$).
  2. Generalized Additive Model with splines (`pygam.LogisticGAM` with s(0) through s(15)).

### Rung L2: Full Environmental Predictor Stack (Linear & Additive)
- **Concept:** Does expanding the feature space with real rainfall, geology, fault distance, pre-event Sentinel-2 NDVI, Sentinel-1 SAR, land cover, and soil improve linear/additive models?
- **Features:** L1 terrain + hydro-meteorological + geological + EO satellite + soil features.
- **Models:**
  1. Regularized Logistic Regression (ElasticNet / L2).
  2. Generalized Additive Model (`LogisticGAM`).

### Rung L3: Tuned Non-Linear Machine Learning & Ensembles
- **Concept:** Do non-linear feature interactions and tree ensembles capture complex slope failure dynamics?
- **Features:** Same L2 environmental stack.
- **Models (Tuned via Nested Spatial Block CV):**
  1. **Random Forest:** `n_estimators` $\in [100, 300]$, `max_depth` $\in [4, 8, \text{None}]$, `min_samples_leaf` $\in [1, 5]$.
  2. **XGBoost:** `max_depth` $\in [3, 5]$, `learning_rate` $\in [0.01, 0.1]$, `scale_pos_weight` tuned to class imbalance.
  3. **LightGBM:** `num_leaves` $\in [15, 31]$, `learning_rate` $\in [0.01, 0.1]$, `min_child_samples` $\in [10, 30]$.
  4. **Support Vector Machine (RBF kernel):** $C \in [0.1, 10.0]$, $\gamma \in [0.01, 1.0]$.
  5. **Standard Tabular MLP:** Linear ($D \to 64 \to 32 \to 1$) with ReLU, LayerNorm, Dropout ($p=0.25$), Focal Loss ($\alpha=0.85, \gamma=3.0$).
  6. **Stacking Ensemble:** Logistic regression meta-learner combining out-of-fold probability outputs from all tuned L3 models.
- **Calibration Protocol:** Every model calibrated using Raw probabilities, Platt scaling (logistic sigmoid), and Isotonic regression.

### Rung L4: Geospatial Foundation Model Embeddings (Prithvi-100M)
- **Concept:** Do self-supervised spatial-temporal representations from a pretrained Earth Observation Vision Transformer provide superior transfer representations compared to hand-engineered features?
- **Foundation Model:** `ibm-nasa-geospatial/Prithvi-EO-1.0-100M` (Vision Transformer ViT, patch size $16 \times 16$, embedding dim 768). Checkpoint weights verifiably loaded from Hugging Face with recorded SHA-256 hash.
- **Input:** $224 \times 224$ pre-event multispectral image chips centered at each sample location (6 optical bands: Blue, Green, Red, Narrow NIR, SWIR-1, SWIR-2).
- **Architecture:** Pretrained Prithvi backbone frozen; extract pooled 768-dimensional spatial-spectral embeddings. Concatenate embeddings with L2 tabular environmental vector into a regularized logistic head or small MLP.
- **Condition:** CPU execution. If chip download or ViT inference times out or fails, marked **`NOT RUN`** with log citation.

### Rung L5: Transfer Learning & Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)
- **Concept:** Does pretraining the classifier on a large open landslide inventory (Landslide4Sense) followed by parameter-efficient Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) on Araku Valley improve spatial generalization?
- **Pretraining Phase:** Train classification backbone on large public benchmark dataset.
- **Adaptation Phase (Araku Valley):** Freeze pretrained backbone weights and adapt Linear projection layers using low-rank decomposition matrices:
  $$W_{adapted} = W_{base} + \frac{\alpha}{r} (B \cdot A), \quad A \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times d_{in}}, B \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{out} \times r}, \quad r=8, \alpha=16$$
- **Benchmark Comparisons:**
  1. Pretrained + LoRA fine-tuning (Proposed).
  2. Pretrained + Frozen backbone (Linear probe).
  3. Pretrained + Full fine-tuning.
  4. Trained from scratch (No pretraining).

### Optional Rung L5b: End-to-End Prithvi LoRA Fine-Tuning
- **Concept:** End-to-end backpropagation through all Prithvi ViT self-attention projection weights using LoRA adapters.
- **Condition:** **GPU ONLY**. Because `torch.cuda.is_available() == False`, L5b is pre-registered and marked **`NOT RUN (No GPU Available)`**.

## 5. Reporting-Bias Control Protocol

Landslide inventories derived from news and civil authorities exhibit severe spatial reporting bias toward linear transportation corridors (KK Railway and SH-39).

1. **Bias Baseline Model:**
   - Train the best L3 model with `dist_to_road_rail_m` included as a predictor.
   - The increase in apparent metric performance ($\Delta \text{PR-AUC}_{bias}$) quantifies the proportion of model skill driven purely by infrastructure proximity.
2. **Headline Model Constraint:**
   - All headline ladder models (L1–L5) strictly exclude road and rail distance from input features.
   - Evaluated against both standard random background negatives and a **bias-matched background negative variant** whose distance distribution to infrastructure mirrors that of verified landslide events.
3. **Road-Adjacent Exclusion Ablation:**
   - Re-evaluate the best model after excluding all events located within 100 m of roads or railways, verifying whether the model retains predictive skill in natural slopes.

## 6. Spatial Cross-Validation & Decision Rule

### 6.1 Spatial Block Partitioning
- **Grid Structure:** Study domain partitioned into 10 km $\times$ 10 km spatial blocks.
- **Block Grouping:** Folds constructed using `GroupKFold(n_splits=5)` grouping by block ID, ensuring that all pixels belonging to an event cluster fall into a single fold.
- **No Test Leakage:** Feature normalizers (StandardScaler) and probability calibrators (Platt / Isotonic) are fitted strictly on the 4 training folds.

### 6.2 Evaluation Metrics
- **Primary Metric:** Precision-Recall Area Under Curve (**PR-AUC**), evaluated alongside baseline random prevalence ($P_{no-skill} = \frac{N_{pos}}{N_{total}}$).
- **Secondary Metrics:**
  - Receiver Operating Characteristic Area Under Curve (**ROC-AUC**).
  - **Brier Score:** $\frac{1}{N} \sum (\hat{p}_i - y_i)^2$.
  - **Brier Skill Score (BSS):** $1 - \frac{\text{Brier}}{p(1-p)}$.
  - **Expected Calibration Error (ECE):** 10-bin reliability diagram error.
  - **Precision at Fixed Recall:** Precision evaluated at 70%, 80%, and 90% recall.
- **Multi-Seed Stability:** All neural and stochastic models evaluated across 10 random seeds (`[42, 101, 202, 303, 404, 505, 606, 707, 808, 909]`), reporting mean and standard deviation.

### 6.3 Statistical Significance & Decision Rule
- **Block-Bootstrap Resampling:** 1,000 bootstrap resamples with replacement drawn at the spatial block level. Bootstrap samples containing 0 positive events are skipped and counted.
- **Paired Difference Test:**
  $$\Delta \text{PR-AUC} = \text{PR-AUC}_{L_k} - \text{PR-AUC}_{L_{best}}$$
- **Decision Criteria:**
  - **Improvement:** 95% bootstrap CI of $\Delta \text{PR-AUC}$ strictly excludes zero ($\text{CI}_{lower} > 0$).
  - **Comparable:** 95% bootstrap CI of $\Delta \text{PR-AUC}$ contains zero.
  - **Worse:** 95% bootstrap CI of $\Delta \text{PR-AUC}$ strictly below zero ($\text{CI}_{upper} < 0$).

## 7. Diagnostics, Uncertainty & Systematic Ablations

1. **Shortcut Diagnostics:**
   - Train a discriminator to predict satellite sensor ID, acquisition season, or processing pipeline from features. If discriminator ROC-AUC $> 0.95$, investigate and eliminate potential data processing shortcuts.
2. **Epistemic Uncertainty Quantification:**
   - 30-pass Monte Carlo Dropout during inference (dropout active with $p=0.25$).
   - Predictive standard deviation ($\sigma_p$) mathematically clamped to $[0, 0.5]$.
   - Correlation between uncertainty and absolute error $|y - \hat{p}|$ evaluated via block-level Spearman rank correlation.
   - **Selective Prediction Curve:** Evaluate error reduction and positive sample retention across uncertainty percentiles (100%, 90%, 80%, 70%, 60%, 50%).
3. **Systematic Ablations Table:**
   - Feature group breakdown (Terrain only vs. EO vs. Hydro vs. Geological).
   - With vs. without pretraining.
   - LoRA vs. full fine-tuning vs. plain MLP.
   - Random background negatives vs. bias-matched negatives vs. steep hard negatives.
   - Exclusion of historical pre-2015 events.

## 8. Susceptibility Mapping & Corridor Exposure

1. **Regional Map Generation:**
   - Generate full-coverage susceptibility probability ($P$) and epistemic uncertainty ($\sigma_p$) GeoTIFFs using the best-performing model rung at native 30 m resolution ($7,200 \times 3,600$ pixels).
   - Mask water bodies and flat terrain ($\text{slope} < 5^\circ$).
2. **Class Area Shares:**
   - Report percentage of regional area in 5 susceptibility classes: Very Low ($<0.05$), Low ($0.05-0.15$), Moderate ($0.15-0.35$), High ($0.35-0.60$), and Very High ($\ge 0.60$).
3. **Infrastructure Corridor Buffers:**
   - Evaluate mean susceptibility and high-risk cell proportion ($P \ge 0.50$) within 250 m and 500 m buffers along the Kottavalasa–Kirandul (KK) railway and State Highway 39 (SH-39).
   - Compare corridor hazard concentration directly against the regional baseline.
4. **Out-of-Fold Event Evaluation:**
   - Report out-of-fold predicted probability and uncertainty at every verified historical event footprint.
